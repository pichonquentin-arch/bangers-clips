Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Data the app uses

  • Basic TikTok profile information (open ID, display name, avatar), to show which account is connected.
  • An access token issued by TikTok, stored only on the author's own computer, used to upload videos.
  • The video files and captions that the author chose to post.

What the app does not do

  • It does not sell or share any data with third parties.
  • It does not collect data about other TikTok users.
  • It has no server and no analytics: everything runs on the author's computer.

Retention and deletion

Video files are moved to the trash after upload. You can revoke access at any time in your TikTok settings, which invalidates the token.
